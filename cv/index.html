Profile

Researcher in human–AI interaction, currently in an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. program. I build LLM- and vision–language-model-based interactive systems — conversational agents for learning, multi-agent personas for live sports co-viewing, an AI docent for museums — and evaluate them with user studies. First author of an EMNLP Findings paper, with two further systems currently under peer review; one system registered as software and one deployed as a public museum exhibit.
